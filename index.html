Sanding Belts FAQ

How do you measure sanding belt length?

Not sure what size sanding belt to order? If you have an existing belt, you can use that to measure for a comparison. For an old/worn belt you don't plan on reusing, cut the belt and measure it end to end. If your only belt is still in working condition, press it flat, measure end to end, and multiply by 2 — that is your sanding belt length.

How do you clean sanding belts?

Like any abrasive product, sanding belts can load up with material through regular use. To extend their life, clean most of the accumulated material with a rubberized cleaning stick, also available on EmpireAbrasives.com.

Can I use sanding belts for wet sanding?

Yes, but not all of them. Some zirconia and ceramic belts, and most silicon carbide grinder belts can be used for dry or wet sanding. Check the product specs to confirm the belt material is waterproof.

Do you sell extra wide sanding belts?

Yes — our wide sanding belts and extra large sanding belts are commonly used with industrial or larger-scale machinery. These range from 25" up to 52" in width, and lengths from 48" up to 103". For other sizes, we also offer custom sanding belt orders.
